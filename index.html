What is Spirit Island?

A Co-op Strategy Game Like No Other

Designed by R. Eric Reuss and published by Greater Than Games in 2017, Spirit Island is a deeply strategic co-operative board game for 1–4 players (up to 6 with expansions). You and your fellow players each control a unique, elemental Spirit of the land, wielding ancient powers to drive colonizing Invaders off your island home before they blight it beyond saving.


Unlike almost every other adventure game, you are not the underdog. You are terrifying forces of nature — lightning, ocean, fire, shadow — and the Invaders are very, very afraid. Or they should be.

Why it's the best

What Makes Spirit Island
Truly Special

🌪️

Radical Asymmetry

Every Spirit plays completely differently. Lightning is fast and reckless. Earth is slow and unyielding. Ocean drowns anything that touches the shore. Mastering even one Spirit feels like learning a whole new game.

🤝

True Co-operation

No one is a passive passenger. Spirits synergize in surprising ways — your powers can power up your allies, call them across the board, or set up devastating combos. Winning together feels genuinely earned.

🔄

Infinite Replayability

Different Spirits, different island layouts, different Invader factions, and multiple difficulty settings mean every game tells a new story. With expansions, you will never run out of things to try.

📈

A Game That Grows with You

Brand new? Pick Lightning or River. Seasoned veteran? Try Fractured Days Split the Sky or Starlight Seeks Its Form. The difficulty dial spans casual afternoon to mind-bending puzzle.

🌍

A Flipped Narrative

Hailed by The Atlantic as one of the few genuinely anti-colonial board games, Spirit Island reframes the classic "explore and conquer" genre entirely. The Invaders are the threat. The land fights back.

🎨

Stunning Production

Gorgeous art, chunky tokens, detailed spirit boards, and a sprawling island map make every session a feast for the eyes. Each Spirit card is a small piece of illustrated mythology.

Spirit Island is complex and deep… an easy recommendation for anyone who can handle the learning curve.

— Ars Technica

The Full Universe

Four Expansions, Endless Depth

Each expansion adds new Spirits, new mechanics, and new ways for the island to fight back. The base game alone is a complete experience — but once you're hooked, the rabbit hole goes deep.

Branch & Claw
Wild Growth

Adds 2 new Spirits, Event cards that make the island feel alive, and new Invader cards. The island itself begins to act — unpredictably.

Jagged Earth
The Big One

The flagship expansion. 10 new Spirits, new Adversaries, Scenarios, and the Badlands terrain. Doubles the game's total content. Essential.

Feather & Flame
Fire & Shadow

4 new Spirits with unique mechanics including the terrifying Heart of the Wildfire and the colossal Serpent Slumbering Beneath the Island.

Nature Incarnate
Godlike Power

8 new Spirits of immense power and complexity. Adds Aspect cards that let you reshape any Spirit's identity. Released 2023.
